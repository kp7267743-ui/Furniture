A little about us

Personal attention.
Beautiful results.

Krishna Kitchen Trolley & Modular Furniture creates kitchens, wardrobes, TV units and bedroom furniture for homes across Nashik. We believe the best spaces are the ones that look beautiful and make daily life easier.

Vinod Prajapati brings personal attention to each project, from the first site visit and measurements to design, fabrication and installation. We come to your home, understand your needs and plan around your space.
